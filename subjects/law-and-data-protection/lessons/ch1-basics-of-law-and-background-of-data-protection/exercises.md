# Exercises

## Chapter 1 MCQ

### Question 1

Which of these is NOT one of the key characteristics of law in the lecture?

- A. Binding nature
- B. Systematic structure
- C. Normative framework
- D. Voluntary compliance

Answer: D. Voluntary compliance. Law is binding and enforceable. The four characteristics are binding nature, systematic structure, social function and normative framework.

---

### Question 2

What best distinguishes the enforcement of law from the enforcement of moral or social rules?

- A. Law relies on social pressure
- B. Law uses formal sanctions such as fines, imprisonment and civil remedies
- C. Law applies only to certain groups
- D. Law is usually unwritten

Answer: B. Law uses formal sanctions such as fines, imprisonment and civil remedies. Violating law brings formal sanctions; other rules rely on social pressure, morality or informal consequences.

---

### Question 3

The role of law that 'enables transactions and economic activity' is the:

- A. Regulatory role
- B. Protective function
- C. Facilitative role
- D. Educational role

Answer: C. Facilitative role. The facilitative role enables transactions and economic activity (e.g. contract law).

---

### Question 4

'People agree to give up some personal freedoms in exchange for the protection and order that laws provide.' This explains obedience through:

- A. Fear of punishment
- B. The social contract
- C. Socialisation
- D. Religious duty

Answer: B. The social contract. This is the social contract idea.

---

### Question 5

According to the lecture, the United Nations framework:

- A. Recognises personal data protection as a fundamental right
- B. Does NOT recognise personal data protection as a fundamental right
- C. Created the GDPR
- D. Established the European Court of Human Rights

Answer: B. Does NOT recognise personal data protection as a fundamental right. The UN protects privacy (UDHR Art. 12, ICCPR) but does not recognise data protection as a separate fundamental right.

---

### Question 6

Under the ECHR, the right to personal data protection forms part of:

- A. Article 6
- B. Article 8
- C. Article 12
- D. Article 17

Answer: B. Article 8. ECHR Article 8 (respect for private life) covers personal data protection. Article 12 is the UDHR provision.

---

### Question 7

The Charter of Fundamental Rights of the EU became legally binding on:

- A. 7 December 2000
- B. 1 December 2009
- C. 25 May 2018
- D. 24 October 1995

Answer: B. 1 December 2009. Adopted in 2000, it became binding EU primary law on 1 December 2009.

---

### Question 8

From 1995 until May 2018, the principal EU data protection instrument was:

- A. Convention 108
- B. The GDPR
- C. Directive 95/46/EC
- D. The ICCPR

Answer: C. Directive 95/46/EC. The Data Protection Directive 95/46/EC applied until the GDPR became applicable on 25 May 2018.

---

### Question 9

Convention 108 was adopted by which organisation?

- A. The United Nations
- B. The Council of Europe
- C. The European Commission
- D. The OECD

Answer: B. The Council of Europe. Convention 108 is a Council of Europe convention on automatic processing of personal data.

---

### Question 10

Which statement about the original treaties of the European Communities is correct?

- A. They contained a full bill of rights
- B. They contained no reference to human rights or their protection
- C. They created the right to be forgotten
- D. They defined personal data

Answer: B. They contained no reference to human rights or their protection. The original treaties did not mention human rights; the Charter filled this gap later.
