# Exercises

## Chapter 6 MCQ

### Question 1

A data subject can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of the Member State of:

- A. The controller's headquarters only
- B. Their habitual residence, place of work, or the alleged infringement
- C. Any EU country, chosen by the SA
- D. The EDPB

Answer: B. Their habitual residence, place of work, or the alleged infringement. These three options.

---

### Question 2

A data subject may bring a court claim against a private controller in the Member State where:

- A. The controller has an establishment, or the data subject habitually resides
- B. Only in Luxembourg
- C. The processor's servers are
- D. Only where the SA decides

Answer: A. The controller has an establishment, or the data subject habitually resides. Either option is available.

---

### Question 3

If an EU institution unlawfully processes personal data, the complaint goes to:

- A. A national court
- B. The General Court of the EU
- C. The ECtHR
- D. The European Commission

Answer: B. The General Court of the EU. The General Court, part of the CJEU.

---

### Question 4

The CJEU may refuse a preliminary ruling request only if:

- A. The answer is not relevant to the original case
- B. The answer would be late
- C. The question is outside its competence
- D. The party is a data subject

Answer: C. The question is outside its competence. It cannot refuse for relevance or timeliness.

---

### Question 5

A not-for-profit body representing data subjects must:

- A. Be a political party
- B. Have public-interest statutory objectives and be active in data protection
- C. Be based outside the EU
- D. Be approved by the controller

Answer: B. Have public-interest statutory objectives and be active in data protection. Both requirements.

---

### Question 6

Anxiety and humiliation caused by illegal processing are:

- A. Material damage
- B. Non-material damage
- C. Not compensable
- D. Administrative fines

Answer: B. Non-material damage. Emotional harm is non-material damage.

---

### Question 7

A processor is liable for damage only where it:

- A. Processed any data
- B. Breached processor-specific obligations or acted outside/contrary to the controller's lawful instructions
- C. Is based outside the EEA
- D. Earned more than €10m

Answer: B. Breached processor-specific obligations or acted outside/contrary to the controller's lawful instructions. Otherwise the controller is liable.

---

### Question 8

Damage is caused by several controllers and processors. Liability is:

- A. Split equally and victims claim each share separately
- B. Each is liable for the entire damage, then can claim back
- C. Only the largest company pays
- D. No one pays

Answer: B. Each is liable for the entire damage, then can claim back. Ensures full and effective compensation.

---

### Question 9

The lower fine tier is up to:

- A. €10 million or 2% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher
- B. €20 million or 4%
- C. €5 million or 1%
- D. €10 million or 2%, whichever is lower

Answer: A. €10 million or 2% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher. For other infringements.

---

### Question 10

Breaching the rules on international transfers falls in which tier?

- A. Lower: €10m or 2%
- B. Higher: €20m or 4%
- C. No fine
- D. Criminal only

Answer: B. Higher: €20m or 4%. Transfers are one of the three higher-tier areas.

---

### Question 11

Which is NOT a corrective power of a supervisory authority?

- A. Issuing orders
- B. Warnings and reprimands
- C. Temporary or permanent processing bans
- D. Imprisoning the company's CEO

Answer: D. Imprisoning the company's CEO. SAs have administrative, not criminal, powers.

---

### Question 12

EU officials who fail their GDPR-related obligations are liable to:

- A. Imprisonment
- B. Disciplinary action
- C. Deportation
- D. Nothing

Answer: B. Disciplinary action. Disciplinary action (Art. 49 as cited).
