# Flashcards

## Card 1

Front:

Art. 9(1) basic rule

Back:

A general prohibition on processing special categories, unless an exception applies.

---

## Card 2

Front:

Why special categories get higher protection

Back:

Processing can significantly affect fundamental rights and freedoms (discrimination, serious harm).

---

## Card 3

Front:

The six exceptions in the lecture

Back:

Explicit consent; complying with EU or Member State law; vital interests; archiving, research, statistics; legal claims; substantial public interest based on law with safeguards.

---

## Card 4

Front:

The 'higher threshold' for special categories

Back:

A lawful basis under Art. 6 AND a separate condition under Art. 9.

---

## Card 5

Front:

Additional transparency for special categories

Back:

Tell people the purpose, the categories of data, and the recipients.

---

## Card 6

Front:

Specific safeguards for special categories

Back:

Pseudonymisation or anonymisation; encryption; access controls; physical security; breach notification; DPIA; Data Protection Officer.

---

## Card 7

Front:

When is biometric data a special category?

Back:

When processed to uniquely identify a person (fingerprints, face, voice).

---

## Card 8

Front:

Art. 10: two ways criminal data may be processed

Back:

Under the control of an official authority, or authorised by EU or Member State law with appropriate safeguards.

---

## Card 9

Front:

Who can keep a comprehensive register of criminal convictions?

Back:

Only an official authority. Private companies cannot.

---

## Card 10

Front:

Three purposes for processing criminal data

Back:

Criminal justice; public safety (national security, terrorism, public health threats); employment (vulnerable people, security clearance).

---

## Card 11

Front:

Retention of criminal record checks by employers

Back:

Only as long as needed for the hiring decision, then erased.

---

## Card 12

Front:

Social worker collects parents' religion for a child at risk. Which exception?

Back:

Vital interests of the data subject or another person.
