# Exercises

## Chapter 5 MCQ

### Question 1

Article 9(1) GDPR:

- A. Allows free processing of sensitive data
- B. Prohibits processing special categories unless an exception applies
- C. Only applies to public authorities
- D. Covers criminal convictions

Answer: B. Prohibits processing special categories unless an exception applies. General prohibition with exceptions.

---

### Question 2

To process special category data lawfully, the controller needs:

- A. Only an Art. 9 exception
- B. Only an Art. 6 basis
- C. Both an Art. 6 lawful basis and an Art. 9 condition
- D. Approval from the EDPB

Answer: C. Both an Art. 6 lawful basis and an Art. 9 condition. The higher threshold: both.

---

### Question 3

A research organisation studies genetic predisposition to disease and asks participants to agree in writing. Which exception?

- A. Explicit consent
- B. Vital interests
- C. Legal claims
- D. Legal obligation

Answer: A. Explicit consent. Explicit consent serves as the legal justification.

---

### Question 4

A social worker collects parents' religious beliefs to assess a child's risk of abuse. Which exception?

- A. Explicit consent
- B. Vital interests
- C. Archiving
- D. Legal claims

Answer: B. Vital interests. The lecture places this under protecting the child's vital interests.

---

### Question 5

A court collects data on a client's ethnicity to establish racial discrimination. Which exception?

- A. Legal claims
- B. Explicit consent
- C. Public health
- D. Statistics

Answer: A. Legal claims. Establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

---

### Question 6

A government agency collects ethnicity data to run a racial-equality programme. Which exception?

- A. Vital interests
- B. Substantial public interest based on law with safeguards
- C. Contract
- D. Legitimate interests

Answer: B. Substantial public interest based on law with safeguards. Public interest based on EU or Member State law.

---

### Question 7

A comprehensive register of criminal convictions may be kept by:

- A. Any employer
- B. Only an official authority
- C. Background-check companies
- D. Insurers

Answer: B. Only an official authority. Art. 10: solely under the control of an official authority.

---

### Question 8

Biometric data becomes a special category when:

- A. It is stored in the cloud
- B. It is processed to uniquely identify a natural person
- C. It is older than one year
- D. It is anonymised

Answer: B. It is processed to uniquely identify a natural person. The purpose of unique identification is the trigger.

---

### Question 9

Which is NOT a purpose for processing criminal data mentioned in the lecture?

- A. Criminal justice
- B. Public safety
- C. Employment checks for vulnerable-people jobs
- D. Targeted advertising

Answer: D. Targeted advertising. Targeted advertising is not a permitted purpose.

---

### Question 10

Additional transparency for special categories requires telling people:

- A. The purpose, categories of data, and recipients
- B. Only the DPO's name
- C. The company's revenue
- D. Nothing extra

Answer: A. The purpose, categories of data, and recipients. Purpose, categories and recipients.
