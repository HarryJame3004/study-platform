# Exercises

## Chapter 7 MCQ

### Question 1

The main GDPR provision on DPIAs is:

- A. Article 25
- B. Article 32
- C. Article 35
- D. Article 49

Answer: C. Article 35. Art. 35; Art. 36 covers prior consultation.

---

### Question 2

A DPIA implements which principles?

- A. Accountability (Art. 5(2)) and privacy by design (Art. 25)
- B. Portability and objection
- C. Consent and contract
- D. Adequacy and derogations

Answer: A. Accountability (Art. 5(2)) and privacy by design (Art. 25). Accountability and privacy by design.

---

### Question 3

Under the EDPB approach, a DPIA should be carried out when processing meets:

- A. Any one criterion
- B. Two or more of the nine criteria
- C. All nine criteria
- D. Five criteria

Answer: B. Two or more of the nine criteria. Two or more.

---

### Question 4

Which is NOT a mandatory DPIA scenario under Art. 35(3)?

- A. Profiling with legal effects
- B. Large-scale processing of health data
- C. Large-scale CCTV of public areas
- D. A small bakery's customer email list

Answer: D. A small bakery's customer email list. A small, low-risk mailing list is not mandatory.

---

### Question 5

In the 8-step methodology, 'Engage DPO and stakeholders' is step:

- A. 2
- B. 4
- C. 6
- D. 8

Answer: C. 6. Step 6: consultation.

---

### Question 6

Prior consultation with the supervisory authority is required when:

- A. Any DPIA is done
- B. High risk cannot be mitigated
- C. The DPO disagrees
- D. Data is anonymised

Answer: B. High risk cannot be mitigated. Art. 36.

---

### Question 7

'Function creep' means:

- A. Faster processing
- B. Processing expanding beyond its original purpose
- C. A type of encryption
- D. A staff training method

Answer: B. Processing expanding beyond its original purpose. A risk event to consider in step 4.

---

### Question 8

Standard contractual clauses and Art. 28 processing agreements are which type of mitigation?

- A. Technical
- B. Physical
- C. Legal or contractual
- D. Organisational

Answer: C. Legal or contractual. Legal/contractual measures.

---

### Question 9

A company plans facial recognition at its office entrance for all employees. The best conclusion is:

- A. No DPIA: employees consented
- B. DPIA required: innovative technology, biometric data and vulnerable (employee) subjects
- C. DPIA only if data leaves the EU
- D. DPIA only after a breach

Answer: B. DPIA required: innovative technology, biometric data and vulnerable (employee) subjects. At least three criteria are met.

---

### Question 10

Which is a DPIA review trigger under Art. 35(11)?

- A. A new logo
- B. A change in risk, such as new technology or a security incident
- C. The DPO's birthday
- D. A fixed 10-year cycle only

Answer: B. A change in risk, such as new technology or a security incident. Review when there is a change in risk.
