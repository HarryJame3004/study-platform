# Flashcards

## Card 1

Front:

Four aims of a DPIA

Back:

Identify and evaluate risks; assess necessity and proportionality; mitigate risks; demonstrate accountability.

---

## Card 2

Front:

Legal basis of a DPIA

Back:

Art. 35 GDPR; WP248 rev.01 guidelines; implements Art. 5(2) accountability and Art. 25 privacy by design.

---

## Card 3

Front:

Three mandatory DPIA scenarios (Art. 35(3))

Back:

Systematic automated evaluation or profiling with significant effects; large-scale special category or criminal data; large-scale systematic monitoring of public areas.

---

## Card 4

Front:

The nine EDPB criteria

Back:

Evaluation or scoring; automated decisions; systematic monitoring; sensitive data; large scale; matching datasets; vulnerable subjects; innovative technology; prevents exercise of rights.

---

## Card 5

Front:

How many EDPB criteria trigger a DPIA?

Back:

Two or more.

---

## Card 6

Front:

The eight DPIA steps

Back:

Screening; description; necessity and proportionality; risk identification; mitigation; consultation; documentation; review.

---

## Card 7

Front:

Elements of the description (Art. 35(7)(a))

Back:

Nature, scope, context, purpose, legal basis, data flows.

---

## Card 8

Front:

Four categories of harm

Back:

Physical, material, non-material, loss of rights.

---

## Card 9

Front:

Four risk events

Back:

Unauthorised access (confidentiality); unauthorised modification (integrity); loss (availability); function creep.

---

## Card 10

Front:

What is function creep?

Back:

Processing gradually expanding beyond its original purpose.

---

## Card 11

Front:

Four categories of mitigation measures

Back:

Technical, organisational, legal or contractual, physical.

---

## Card 12

Front:

When is prior consultation with the SA needed?

Back:

Art. 36: when high risk cannot be mitigated.

---

## Card 13

Front:

DPIA review triggers (Art. 35(11))

Back:

Change in risk: changes in operations, new technologies, security incidents.

---

## Card 14

Front:

Eight DPIA quality criteria

Back:

Systematic, comprehensive, proportionate, documented, consultative, actionable, accountable, dynamic.
