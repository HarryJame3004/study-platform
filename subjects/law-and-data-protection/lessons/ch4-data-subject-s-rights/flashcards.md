# Flashcards

## Card 1

Front:

The eight data subject rights

Back:

Informed; access; rectification; erasure; restriction; portability; object; rights on automated decision-making and profiling.

---

## Card 2

Front:

Qualities the information must have

Back:

Concise, transparent, intelligible, easily accessible, clear and plain language; without excessive delay or expense.

---

## Card 3

Front:

When to inform if data comes from the data subject

Back:

At the time the data are obtained (Art. 13).

---

## Card 4

Front:

When to inform if data comes from other sources

Back:

Within a reasonable period, at the latest within one month, or before disclosure to a third party (Art. 14).

---

## Card 5

Front:

Five methods for giving privacy information

Back:

Layered notices; dashboards; just-in-time notices; icons; mobile and smart device features.

---

## Card 6

Front:

What must be explained about automated decision-making?

Back:

The logic involved, its significance, and the envisaged consequences.

---

## Card 7

Front:

What the right of access provides

Back:

Confirmation whether data is processed; a copy of the data; supplementary information.

---

## Card 8

Front:

Right to rectification

Back:

Correct inaccurate data without undue delay; complete incomplete data, including by a supplementary statement.

---

## Card 9

Front:

What happens to processing while accuracy is contested?

Back:

It should be restricted during verification, whether or not restriction was requested.

---

## Card 10

Front:

Manifestly unfounded requests

Back:

No real intention (withdraw in return for a benefit); malicious or harassing; unsubstantiated; targeting an employee; systematic campaigns.

---

## Card 11

Front:

Excessive requests

Back:

Repeat the substance of previous requests, or overlap with other requests.

---

## Card 12

Front:

Six grounds for erasure (Art. 17)

Back:

No longer necessary; consent withdrawn with no other basis; objection with no overriding grounds; unlawful processing; legal obligation to erase; children's information society services data.

---

## Card 13

Front:

Six exceptions to erasure

Back:

Freedom of expression and information; legal obligation; public task or official authority; public health; archiving, research, statistics; legal claims.

---

## Card 14

Front:

Google Spain (C-131/12, 2014)

Back:

A search engine indexing content is a controller and can be required to remove links to outdated personal information, balancing the public's interest in information.

---

## Card 15

Front:

Brunet v France

Back:

ECtHR: keeping details of a man whose criminal case was discontinued in a police database for 20 years, with no way to delete, violated Art. 8.

---

## Card 16

Front:

Duty after erasure of published data

Back:

Take reasonable steps (considering technology and cost) to inform other controllers processing the data.

---

## Card 17

Front:

Four grounds for restriction (Art. 18)

Back:

Accuracy contested; unlawful but person prefers restriction; needed for legal claims; objection pending.

---

## Card 18

Front:

Three methods to restrict data

Back:

Move it temporarily to another system; make it unavailable to users; temporarily remove it.

---

## Card 19

Front:

What may be done with restricted data?

Back:

Only storage, unless consent; legal claims; protecting another person's rights; important public interest.

---

## Card 20

Front:

Two conditions for data portability

Back:

Basis is consent or contract AND processing is by automated means.

---

## Card 21

Front:

Required format for portability

Back:

Structured, commonly used and machine-readable; transmitted directly controller to controller where technically feasible.

---

## Card 22

Front:

When does the general right to object apply?

Back:

When the basis is public task or legitimate interests, including profiling.

---

## Card 23

Front:

Objection to direct marketing

Back:

Any time, free of charge, absolute. Must be told clearly and separately from other information.

---

## Card 24

Front:

Information society service

Back:

Normally provided for remuneration, at a distance, by electronic means, at the individual request of a recipient.

---

## Card 25

Front:

When is automated decision-making with significant effects allowed?

Back:

Necessary for a contract; explicit consent; or authorised by law with safeguards.
