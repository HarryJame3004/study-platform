# Exercises

## Chapter 4 MCQ

### Question 1

If personal data is obtained from another source, the data subject must be informed:

- A. Within 72 hours
- B. Within a reasonable period, at the latest within one month
- C. Within one year
- D. Only if they ask

Answer: B. Within a reasonable period, at the latest within one month. Art. 14: reasonable period, at the latest one month, or before disclosure to a third party.

---

### Question 2

The right to data portability applies only when:

- A. Processing is on paper
- B. The basis is consent or contract and processing is by automated means
- C. The basis is legal obligation
- D. The data is anonymised

Answer: B. The basis is consent or contract and processing is by automated means. Both conditions must be met.

---

### Question 3

Data for portability must be provided in a format that is:

- A. PDF only
- B. Structured, commonly used and machine-readable
- C. Printed and signed
- D. Encrypted with the controller's key

Answer: B. Structured, commonly used and machine-readable. The GDPR requires structured, commonly used, machine-readable formats but no specific one.

---

### Question 4

The general right to object applies when the lawful basis is:

- A. Consent or contract
- B. Public task or legitimate interests
- C. Legal obligation
- D. Vital interests

Answer: B. Public task or legitimate interests. Art. 21(1): public task or legitimate interests, including profiling.

---

### Question 5

A person objects to direct marketing. The controller:

- A. May continue if it has compelling grounds
- B. Must stop; the objection is free of charge and can be made at any time
- C. May charge a fee
- D. Must first run a DPIA

Answer: B. Must stop; the objection is free of charge and can be made at any time. The right to object to direct marketing is absolute.

---

### Question 6

In Google Spain (C-131/12), the CJEU held that:

- A. Search engines are only intermediaries
- B. A search engine operator is a controller and can be required to delist results
- C. Newspapers must delete archives
- D. The right to be forgotten doesn't exist

Answer: B. A search engine operator is a controller and can be required to delist results. When indexing content, a search engine becomes a controller.

---

### Question 7

A person repeatedly submits requests with the same substance. The request may be refused as:

- A. Manifestly unfounded
- B. Excessive
- C. Unlawful
- D. Anonymous

Answer: B. Excessive. Repeating the substance of previous requests makes it excessive.

---

### Question 8

A person offers to withdraw a request if the company pays them. The request is:

- A. Excessive
- B. Manifestly unfounded
- C. Valid
- D. Portable

Answer: B. Manifestly unfounded. No real intention to exercise the right.

---

### Question 9

While the controller checks whether contested data is accurate, processing should be:

- A. Continued normally
- B. Restricted
- C. Erased
- D. Transferred

Answer: B. Restricted. Restriction applies during verification.

---

### Question 10

Which is NOT an exception to the right to erasure?

- A. Freedom of expression and information
- B. Establishment or defence of legal claims
- C. Reasons of public health
- D. The controller's interest in marketing

Answer: D. The controller's interest in marketing. Marketing interest is not an exception.

---

### Question 11

Automated decisions with significant effects are allowed when:

- A. The controller saves money
- B. Necessary for a contract, with explicit consent, or authorised by law with safeguards
- C. The decision is always accurate
- D. The person doesn't object within a week

Answer: B. Necessary for a contract, with explicit consent, or authorised by law with safeguards. These are the three permitted situations.

---

### Question 12

Which is NOT a method of restricting processing?

- A. Temporarily moving data to another system
- B. Making data unavailable to users
- C. Temporarily removing published data
- D. Permanently deleting the data

Answer: D. Permanently deleting the data. Permanent deletion is erasure, not restriction.

---

### Question 13

A 'just-in-time' notice is:

- A. A notice sent a year later
- B. Focused privacy information given at the moment data is collected
- C. A court order
- D. A breach notification

Answer: B. Focused privacy information given at the moment data is collected. Delivered when you collect the information.

---

### Question 14

After erasing data that it had made public, a controller must:

- A. Do nothing more
- B. Take reasonable steps to inform other controllers processing that data
- C. Pay compensation
- D. Notify the police

Answer: B. Take reasonable steps to inform other controllers processing that data. Considering available technology and implementation cost.
