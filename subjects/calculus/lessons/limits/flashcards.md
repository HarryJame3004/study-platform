# Calculus Limits - Flashcards

## Q: What is a sequence?
A: An ordered list of numbers \(a_1,a_2,a_3,...\).

## Q: What does convergence mean?
A: A sequence converges if its limit exists and is a finite real number.

## Q: What does divergence mean?
A: A sequence diverges if it has no finite limit.

## Q: What is the dominant term trick?
A: Divide numerator and denominator by the highest power of n.

## Q: When can we use the geometric limit rule?
A: If \(|r|<1\), then \(r^n\to0\).

## Q: State the Squeeze Theorem.
A: If \(a_n\le b_n\le c_n\) and both outside sequences have the same limit L, then \(b_n\to L\).

## Q: Why use Limit Laws?
A: To split complicated limits into simpler limits.
