# Calculus Limits - Exercises

## Practice 1: Limit Laws

Compute:

1.
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}(\frac1n+7)
\]

2.
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}(5-\frac2n)
\]

3.
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{3n+1}{n}
\]

---

## Practice 2: Dominant Term

Compute:

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{3n^2+2n+1}{5n^2+7}
\]

Hint: Divide numerator and denominator by the highest power of n.

---

## Practice 3: Convergence

Decide whether each sequence converges or diverges:

1. \(a_n=\frac1n\)
2. \(a_n=n^2\)
3. \(a_n=(-1)^n\)
